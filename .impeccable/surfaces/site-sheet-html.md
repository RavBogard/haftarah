# Surface: the web sheet (site/sheet.html, site/index.html)

Scope: the week page and the landing page at haftarah.centralreform.org. Mode: Read.
Audience: people looking a sheet up later, Zoom participants, iPad readers at the table.
Job: read the whole haftarah with its commentary, open any source, look up any word.
Constraints: inherit DESIGN.md (the Critical Edition) on a screen; no framework; data from Blob; no change to the printed sheet.
Chosen direction: layout A (text in two columns, commentary rail that keeps pace). Memorable moment: the rail and running head move with the reader; a word opens its root.
Unresolved: landing-page welcome copy (Daniel approves wording); mobile order Hebrew above English (Daniel can flip).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The printed critical edition, unbound: the same page grammar (scripture owns the column, notes keyed by letter, apparatus beneath) rearranged so the apparatus travels with the reader instead of waiting at the foot of a page.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The sheet's own front page: mural, fitted incipit, reading line in rubric, opening note at a book measure. Nothing introduces the sheet; the sheet introduces itself.
VISITOR PATH: Front matter, then the reading with the rail keeping pace; a key letter opens its note in place; a source name opens the full passage; a Hebrew word opens its root; the end matter closes with the next-week line; the landing page lists every week.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The running head and the rail follow the scroll (the print running head reinterpreted); any Hebrew word opens its root and dictionary senses from Sefaria.
CROSS-SURFACE REACH: The landing page is the series: the same masthead, the mural, the newest week set like a hero, the index as a ruled list. Dark mode and text size persist across both.
HONEST RISK: Four tracks of information (English, Hebrew, notes, apparatus) on one screen can crowd; the rail must stay quiet (one bar, no boxes) and the phone fold must hide nothing keyed.
