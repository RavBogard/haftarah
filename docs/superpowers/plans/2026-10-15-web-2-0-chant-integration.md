## 2. Two facts checked today that change the design

- **Range requests pass through the rewrite (O5).** `curl -H "Range: bytes=0-1"` against `https://haftarah.vercel.app/data/weeks/2026-10-17-noach/sheet.pdf` returns `206 Partial Content`, `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, `Content-Range: bytes 0-1/877133`, the same as the store's own URL. An `/audio/` rewrite of the same form will serve Safari. No direct-origin fallback in `media-src` is needed.
- **Blob's Hobby allowance is per account, not per store (O6).** Vercel's Blob pricing page (2026-09-23): "The included resource usage for the Hobby plan is shared across all Vercel services in your project"; "Splitting the same data across more stores doesn't change those costs"; "you will not be able to access Vercel Blob if limits are exceeded … wait until 30 days have passed". So the 2.0 spec's reason for a second store (a Hobby block on audio could not take the week data down) does not hold. A second store still earns its keep for listing, garbage collection, a separate token and never-overwrite uploads, and it costs one Advanced Operation. **Daniel confirmed on 2026-10-15 that the team is on Vercel Pro**, so the Hobby block never applies: Blob is usage-based against the plan's monthly credit (storage about $0.023 per GB-month, transfer about $0.05 per GB in iad1 per Vercel's pricing page). The typical haftarah is about 2.8 MB at 64 kbps; the whole haftarah cycle in one option about 180 MB (about $0.04 a month to store); five options about 900 MB (about $0.21). A cycle backfill is affordable; what to watch is transfer, at 2.8 MB per full listen. Spend Management on the team (a notification at, say, $10 of usage) is the one setting worth turning on before audio ships. This settles D3, D5 and D13's plan question and 2.0 open item 3.

## 7. Risks and notes for the merge

- `trope/hebcal.mjs` and `scripts/fetch.mjs` both call Hebcal's leyning endpoint with their own parsers. They can coexist in Wave 5; folding the engine's reading flags onto `readings[].aliyot` (the trope spec's Wave 1 remainder) is the natural point to make `fetch.mjs` the single caller.
- `npm test` grows by the trope suite; the Wave 1 golden is untouched by the branch (no `template/` file changes).
- The branch's handoff says it must never be rebased onto main and that another coder pushes to main. The merge is a plain `git merge` (or a PR) by the web side at Wave 5's start, after Daniel's sessions have pushed their last commits.
- Nothing in `trope/env/` or `haftarah-audio` may be read by a test or a publish step; the allow-list test in the branch's `trope/chant.mjs` (no drive letter, no backslash, no `haftarah-audio`, `prompt` or `model` in a public manifest) is the gate, and publish's side-file upload must refuse a `chant.json` that fails it.
