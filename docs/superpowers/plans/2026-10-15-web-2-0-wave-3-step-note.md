# Courtesy note to STEPBible (for Daniel to send)

To: STEPBibleATGmail.com (the address in the TAHOT file header)
Subject: Using TAHOT and TBESH on a congregation's weekly study site

Hello,

I'm the rabbi at Central Reform Congregation in St. Louis. Our weekly haftarah and Torah study sheets have a small public web version, haftarah.centralreform.org, for the adults in our Torah from Scratch class, many of whom join remotely.

We have started using your TAHOT and TBESH data (CC BY 4.0) for a word panel: a tap on a Hebrew word shows a plain-English description of what it is doing, its prefixes and suffixes, where the same dictionary word occurs in the reading, and how often it occurs in the Hebrew Bible, with a link to STEPBible for the whole-Tanakh search. The credit "Data created by www.STEPBible.org based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge (CC BY 4.0)" appears on every page that shows it and in the project's README.

We do not redistribute your files. What our site publishes is a trimmed, per-week derived file (the week's few hundred words, aligned to Sefaria's Miqra according to the Masorah text, with the divine name set as יי as our sheet prints it) and a table of counts per dictionary word; both carry the credit and a note of those changes, as your licence asks.

Thank you for making the data available. If anything here should be done differently, please tell me.

Rabbi Daniel Bogard
Central Reform Congregation, St. Louis
