Torah from Scratch

Haftarah

Every week at Torah from Scratch we read the haftarah, the passage from the Prophets that follows the Torah reading. These sheets are how we study it: the Hebrew with its cantillation, the JPS translation beside it, and commentary in three registers: the classical commentators, modern ones, and what historians say. Open any Hebrew word for its root, or any source to read it in full on Sefaria.

Rabbi Daniel Bogard, Central Reform Congregation
